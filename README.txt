BIZCAFE PRESENTATION — OFFLINE WEBSITE

HOW TO OPEN
1. Unzip the downloaded ZIP.
2. Open the BizCafe-Presentation folder in PyCharm on your MacBook.
3. Double-click index.html (or right-click > Open in Browser).
4. On the HP work laptop, copy the ENTIRE folder using an IT-approved method.
5. Open index.html with Chrome or Microsoft Edge. No PyCharm or Python required.

EDITING
- index.html: Text and layout.
- style.css: Colours, typography, effects, and animations.
- script.js: Change CAFE_NAME and update the 10 rounds under 'rounds'.
- The logos and scorecard are placeholders. They are NOT real team information.
- All assets are embedded or local, so no network connection is required.

PRESENTING
- Use browser full-screen (F11 on many Windows laptops).
- Click section buttons or use left/right arrow keys to navigate sections.
- Click decision choices to reveal the placeholder team choice.

IMPORTANT
Ask IT whether local HTML/JavaScript files and file transfers are allowed on your work laptop. Test on the exact laptop/browser before your presentation.

UPDATE: Café name is now Beans & Bloom. The Journey screen includes a 3-stage teamwork timeline and round-by-round development captions. All decision options and scores remain examples/placeholders, and all 3 logos remain placeholder art.
